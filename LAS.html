PROBLEM
Why this problem?

Intelligence analysts face rapidly changing tools and data, making it difficult to keep up with evolving technology. They often take notes but sharing is rare, leading to knowledge silos and inefficient collaboration.

Does the problem actually exist?

To gain deeper insights into the problem, I conducted interviews with 6 target digital network analysts from NSA to understand their daily challenges and workflow pain points.

Conducted 6 analyst interviews

Competitive analysis of existing tools

💡 Research Insights
Analysts have multiple tools but need a unified solution to integrate them. Their current tools lack cross-platform compatibility and efficient collaboration features.

What are the main pain points identified?

Through user interviews and competitive analysis, I identified three key problem areas that were impacting analysts' efficiency and collaboration.

#1 Multiple Tools

Switching multiple tools disrupt workflow and causes inefficiency in daily operations.

#2 Inefficient Collaboration

Poor knowledge sharing between senior and junior analysts hinders mentorship.

#3 Unorganized Documentation

Important queries and findings are scattered and difficult to find when needed.

Compliance

How do we ensure that SPINE meets government compliance requirements? What does the experience look like for different user roles?

Solution: SPINE

Based on our research and collaboration, I developed SPINE - an AI-powered system that enhances the mentoring process and automates documentation for intelligence analysts.

Goal Setting

Senior analysts can assign projects and set goals for team members with AI-driven suggestions for datasets.

Natural Language Queries

Convert natural language prompts to database queries with adjustable time, date, and area ranges.

Outlier Detection

Track and assess performance of multiple analysts simultaneously to identify tradecraft tendencies.

Automated Reports

Generate standardized reports from queries for daily submission to higher-ups using AI.
